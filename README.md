# Business Intelligence Engine for Bank Loan Portfolios Analytics

## Project Overview

An interactive **Bank Loan & Debt Monitoring Dashboard** built with **Power BI (`DAX`, `Power Query`)** and **Microsoft Excel (`Financial_loan_data.xlsx`)** to monitor loan portfolio performance, borrower repayment health, and credit risk exposure across **`38.6K` loans (`$435.8M` funded)**.

## Key Portfolio Metrics

* **Total Loan Applications:** `38.6K` (`MTD: 4.3K` | `MoM: +6.9%`)
* **Total Funded Amount:** `$435.8M` (`MTD: $54.0M` | `MoM: +13.0%`)
* **Total Amount Received:** `$473.1M` (`MTD: $58.1M` | `MoM: +15.8%`)
* **Average Interest Rate & DTI:** `12.0%` Interest Rate | `13.3%` Debt-to-Income (DTI)
* **Good vs. Bad Loan Split:** **`86.2%` Good Loans** (`Fully Paid` + `Current`: `$370.2M` funded, `$435.8M` received) vs. **`13.8%` Bad Loans** (`Charged Off`: `$65.5M` funded, `$37.3M` received)

## 1. Summary Dashboard

Tracks portfolio-level KPIs, **Good Loan vs. Bad Loan** ratios, and the **Loan Status Grid** (`Fully Paid`, `Current`, and `Charged Off`), with interactive filters by **Credit Grade (`A–G`)**, **State**, and **Loan Purpose**.
